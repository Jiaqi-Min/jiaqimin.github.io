Biography

I am a Ph.D. student in Mathematical Statistics at Central University of Finance and Economics, advised by Prof. Yuehan Yang.

I received my M.Sc. in Statistics from Beijing Technology and Business University in 2026, advised by Prof. Zhi Geng and Associate Prof. Shanshan Luo. I received my B.Sc. in Statistics from Central South University in 2022.

Research Interests

Causal Inference

During my master's studies, I worked on identifying and estimating causal effects from observational data. I am now exploring the design and analysis of randomized experiments, with a particular interest in causal inference for finite populations.

Randomized Experiments

Experimental design, covariate adjustment, inference based on randomization, and spillover effects

Observational Studies

Proximal causal inference, bidirectional causal effects, instrumental variables, missing data, and data fusion
